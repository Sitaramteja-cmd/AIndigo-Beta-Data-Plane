Step-by-Step Onboarding & AI Agent Integration Guide

Follow these 4 steps to route your custom AI agents, RAG pipelines, or IDEs through AIndigo's local Data Plane proxy.

Step 1: Pair Your Local Data Plane

Open the 1. Node Pairing & Auth tab and sign up with your work email. This links your local Data Plane proxy (http://127.0.0.1:8080) to your Cloud Control Plane account using a cryptographic Cluster Sync Secret.

Step 2: Swap Raw LLM Key for Virtual Key

Open the 2. Virtual Key Vault tab. Paste your actual Google AI Studio, OpenAI, or Anthropic API key. AIndigo encrypts your real key in its local AES-256-GCM vault and gives you a safe vk-aindigo-... Virtual Key.

Step 3: Point Your AI Agent Base URL to 8080

You do not need to route all corporate network traffic through AIndigo. Only set your AI agent or SDK's BASE_URL to http://127.0.0.1:8080/v1 and pass your vk-aindigo-... key.

Step 4: Multi-Agent & SIEM Monitoring

One Data Plane handles dozens of different AI agents and service accounts simultaneously—just mint a unique Virtual Key per agent. Export or stream all compliance receipts from the Security & SIEM Logs tab.

1. Reversible Ephemeral Crypto-Vaulting (DLP)

Before a prompt leaves your perimeter, sensitive credentials, PII, and internal hostnames are replaced with realistic synthetic placeholders (`AKIA0000VAULTKEY0001`). When the LLM responds, AIndigo re-hydrates the real values seamlessly.

2. Zero-Knowledge Merkle Audit Chain

Every AI turn is hashed into an HMAC-SHA256 Merkle chain (`previous_receipt_hash` → `receipt_hmac`). Security auditors and SIEM platforms get tamper-evident cryptographic proof of governance without storing proprietary source code in the cloud.

3. Non-Intrusive Infrastructure Placement

Deployed as a lightweight sidecar or internal VPC container (`ai-gateway.internal.corp:8080`). Normal corporate web traffic bypasses AIndigo; only outbound AI agent calls route through the gateway.

4. Multi-Agent & Multi-Provider Scalability

A single Data Plane instance manages hundreds of upstream LLM keys and isolated departmental Virtual Keys across Google Gemini, Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, and AWS Bedrock.
